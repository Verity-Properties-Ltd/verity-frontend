iii.

Four states.

The page surfaces three interactive states beyond the default: switching between buyer and developer waitlist forms, and the post-submit confirmation. All three are shown scrolled to the waitlist section.

4 states
A · Default · top of page 1440 · scroll = 0
B · Buyer waitlist form tab = buyer · scrolled
C · Developer Founding 50 form tab = developer · scrolled
D · Submitted · confirmation post-submit · scrolled
— Notes · Tab switching is instant (no transition). Form submit is intercepted client-side and swaps the active form for the confirmation panel — no network request fires in this preview build.
