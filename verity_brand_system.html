xiv. Motion · easing, duration, restraint Registrar tempo · ≤ 280ms

Verity moves like a registrar — measured, deliberate, never showy. Transitions exist to acknowledge action, not perform it. If a movement competes with the content, it is wrong.

— Principles —
— RESTRAINED

No bounce. No spring overshoot. The brand does not gesture for attention.

— PURPOSEFUL

Every transition acknowledges a state change. Decoration is forbidden.

— OPT-OUT

Always honor prefers-reduced-motion. Replace movement with crossfade ≤ 80ms.

Domain glossary · vocabulary of record

— These are the canonical terms. Use them in product, marketing, and legal copy. Never substitute startup synonyms ("user" for "holder", "post" for "issue", "trust score" for "confidence band").

folio
The hex identifier of a credential of record. Always shown with the 0x prefix.
holder
The party recognized as in lawful possession of a credential. Never "user", never "owner".
issuer
The authority who sealed and signed a credential at issuance — a registry, bank, university, ministry.
witness
A third party who countersigns a credential. Three witnesses unlock the highest tier.
chain of authority
The unbroken sequence of issuance, transfer, reissue and witness events for one credential.
anchor
The cryptographic fingerprint (ED25519) written to the public ledger at issuance.
seal
The act of finalizing a credential at issuance. A sealed credential cannot be silently amended.
reissue
The replacement of a sealed credential, recorded as its own ledger event.
revoke
Withdrawal by the issuer. Verification fails after a revocation; the prior chain remains visible.
of record
The phrase used in headlines and certificates. "The holder of record", "the issuer of record".
witnessed
The highest confidence tier — issuer signed, ledger anchored, three witnesses.
confidence band
The five-tier visual gauge that appears on every credential. The brand's signature pattern.
